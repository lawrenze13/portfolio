Concept A

Latent Space: your work as an embedding you can fly through

The idea

Every project, tool and job is a glowing point, placed by what it's about, the way an AI model places text. Related work clusters: AI & agents, platforms, quality, interface. The camera drifts through the cloud; your avatar is a small figure of light that leads the way.

The five stops

  • Homethe whole cloud, slowly rotating
  • Aboutfly into your "core": the densest point
  • Patha glowing trail through the points, 2018 → now
  • Workclusters open into case studies
  • Signalcontact as a beam out of the cloud

How it shows AI engineering

Ask the guide is retrieval made visible. A question becomes a beam; the nearest points light up with their similarity scores, and the camera flies to the best match. Visitors watch retrieval-augmented answering happen.
