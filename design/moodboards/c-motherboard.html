Concept C

Motherboard City: a circuit board you can walk on

The idea

The site is a giant circuit board seen from above, like a city. Chips are projects, grouped by zone; data pulses along copper traces between them. A big AI core sits in the middle, wired to everything. You're a tiny figure walking the traces.

The five stops

  • Bootthe whole board powering on, trace by trace
  • CPUabout you: the processor socket
  • MemoryRAM sticks as your timeline, 2018 → now
  • Chipsproject chips lift off the board into case studies
  • I/Ocontact through the ports

How it shows AI engineering

The AI core is the hub. Ask a question and a signal leaves the core, runs the traces to the matching chips and lights them. The terminal doubles as the ask box, so typed commands feel native.
