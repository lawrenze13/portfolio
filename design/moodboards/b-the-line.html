Concept B

The Line: your career as a transit map you can ride

The idea

A tabletop diorama of a transit network. The AI line and the Platform line cross at Central (About me); a black career line runs 2018 → now. Each station is a project. You drive the train; the camera rides along.

The five stops

  • Homethe whole map, tilted, a train passing
  • AboutCentral interchange, your station
  • The lineride the career line, one station per job
  • Projectsride either line; stations open into case studies
  • Terminuscontact, with a ticket to take away (your CV)

How it shows AI engineering

"Plan my route" is Ask the guide. A visitor types what they're hiring for; the guide plans a route through the stations that match, explains each stop, and the train rides it.
